##### Nigels hairdressing

Hello my name is Nigel Ashtonhurst a mobile hairdresser located in Rochdale and Wigan .I have been a hairdresser for the past 37 years. I specialise in precision haircutting, short hairwork , texturizing techniques and work well with curly hair.
