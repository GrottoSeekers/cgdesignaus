## Anatomy of a Highly Efficient Sales Page (his brief)

- **Top bar**: small amount of branding if essential. Don't take up space with a photo.
- **Heading**: a clear, compelling, specific promise. Avoid cheesy phrases.
- **Subheading**: the "uppercut" — adds context to the promise.
- **VSL**: 1–3 minutes, no script, no fancy edits. Say what it is, why they need it, what they get.
  Skip testimonials — a bold money-back guarantee builds trust faster.
- **Pricing**: no calls, no consults — direct checkout. Email or phone for questions, not required.
- **Footer**: privacy policy and T&Cs optional. Do NOT include social links (they send prospects
  back up the funnel).

Golden rules: make the first thing they see impactful; no redundant text; show your pricing and be
transparent; no sales calls (swap them for a money-back guarantee); let the page do the selling —
your job is to send traffic to it.
